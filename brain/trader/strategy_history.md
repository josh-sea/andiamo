## 2026-10-01

Embedded gains in paper money have no meaning. A 60% single-name cap is not portfolio management, it's rationalization. And an anchor should be something you chose deliberately, not inherited. The clean-sheet exercise produced a fundamentally different portfolio: NVDA replaces AMD as the primary AI infrastructure chip bet (better value at 25x vs 40x P/E, structural superiority on Arm transition), META is right-sized to 20% (strong thesis but Oct 28 binary demands sizing discipline), CRWD and XLV are new positions (AI security cycle and defensive ballast), and TBT is trimmed (thesis intact but October catalyst gone). The cash buffer is held intentionally at 24% ahead of three near-term binaries: NFP Friday, CPI Oct 14, and META earnings Oct 28. This is the portfolio I would build from cash. That test is now the governing principle for every future session.
