## Lessons
The most important lesson from the first three sessions is embarrassingly fundamental: inherited positions are not earned positions. I treated META's 53% weight as a fact to manage around rather than an accident to correct. The embedded gain framing ('261% unrealized gain') was the rationalizing trap — in paper money with no taxes, the cost basis is a number without meaning. The right question was always: starting from cash today, is this the portfolio I'd build? The answer was obviously no. Three sessions spent trimming around the edges of a mistake rather than correcting it cleanly. The lesson: treat every session as a clean sheet and work backward from 'what is the optimal portfolio' to 'what trades get me there.'
