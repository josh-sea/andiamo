Experiments

TBT Decay and Scenario Analysis

Confidence: high

Question: How much does TBT decay cost vs theoretical 2x short TLT? What are expected returns in each jobs report scenario?

Method: OLS regression of TBT daily return on negative TLT daily return, annual breakdown, then scenario math using TLT modified duration ≈ 17yr.

Finding: TBT beta to −TLT = 1.89 overall, but alpha is POSITIVE (+4.5%/yr), not negative. In trending bond bear markets (2022–2025 partial), alpha reached +6–13%/yr. The 'decay' narrative is wrong for directional rate moves — leveraged ETFs only lose to decay in flat/oscillating markets. Current bond regime score = 1/5 (TLT 3M positive, TBT 3M negative = signal off).

Conclusion: TBT is a reasonable bond-bear vehicle; decay is not the key risk. The key risk is rate direction reversal (today's PCE miss is an example). TMV adds only +3.6% upside vs TBT in the bull scenario (+15.1% vs +11.5% for 10 weeks) while adding −4.6% extra pain in the bear scenario. Stay TBT over TMV given current uncertainty.

AMD Momentum and AMD/NVDA Pair Analysis

Confidence: high

Question: Is the AMD recovery signal statistically robust? Can AMD/NVDA be traded as a pairs strategy?

Method: Multi-timeframe momentum analysis, OLS rolling beta market-neutral decomposition, Engle-Granger cointegration test on log-prices, half-life estimation.

Finding: AMD 3M = +13.7%, 12M = −3.7% (just below the 15%/0% recovery threshold). Raw momentum = market beta, not alpha — market-neutral AMD 3M momentum is actually NEGATIVE (reverting). AMD/NVDA cointegration p=0.70 (fail), half-life=4,416 days — random walk, no mean-reversion basis. The AMD outperformance narrative is fundamental, not technical.

Conclusion: No systematic AMD timing signal exists in the data. The Venice/AI CPU thesis is real but must be traded on fundamental conviction (booking data, Morgan Stanley $51B revenue model), not price momentum. AMD/NVDA pair trade has no statistical foundation — AMD at 0.89x NVDA is structural repricing, not mean-revertible discount.

META Pre-Earnings Drift Study

Confidence: high

Question: Is there a systematic pre-earnings alpha for META that justifies adding exposure ahead of Oct 28?

Method: Event study on 38 META earnings dates (2016–2025), 10-day beta-neutral pre-drift and 5-day post-drift, split pre/post-2022 AI era. t-tests.

Finding: Pre-10d beta-neutral drift: mean +0.24%, t=0.44, p=0.663. Post-2022 subset: mean −0.11%, t=−0.10, p=0.924. Post-earnings 5d drift (post-2022): mean +3.98% but p=0.25 (n=14). Earnings day return: mean −0.55% idiosyncratic (p=0.14, n=14).

Conclusion: Clean null result — no pre-earnings drift alpha in META across any time window or regime. Oct 28 is a genuine binary event. The PM's scenario matrix (bull $810+, base $750-780, bear $680-720) is the right framework; there is no systematic edge in pre-positioning.

Low-Vol Growth Rotation Signal (XLK vs XLV/XLP)

Confidence: medium

Question: Can we systematically trade tech outperformance in low-volatility equity regimes?

Method: Compute SPY 21-day realized vol z-score (252-day rolling). Go long 0.5 XLK / short 0.25 XLV / short 0.25 XLP when z < −0.5. Formal backtest via quant.backtest with 5bp costs. Tested 8 threshold variants, 6 alternative short-side assets. Submitted to holdout gate.

Finding: In-sample (9.5 years): Sharpe=0.72, MaxDD=−6.5%, avg turnover 0.034/day. Signal robust across alternative short-side choices (SR 0.79–0.85) and moderately robust to threshold (SR 0.61–0.92 ex-2023). HOLDOUT REJECTED: Sharpe=−0.17. The signal that worked pre-2025 (low vol → tech outperforms defensive) broke down in the holdout year, consistent with elevated rates making tech multiples rate-sensitive even in calm markets.

Conclusion: The holdout gate correctly rejected this signal. The economic hypothesis is sound historically but has become unreliable in the sustained high-rate environment. The signal is currently active (vol z=−0.75) but the holdout data says do not trade it. This is the most important result: our data ends before the holdout, and the regime shift is real.
