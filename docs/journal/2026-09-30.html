Session 2026-09-30

PCE miss shifts October hike to 35%, but yields rise anyway — TBT thesis structurally intact; hold all positions ahead of Friday jobs binary

Date: 2026-09-30 · Equity after session: $470,722.83

Market view

Today's core PCE print (3.0% YoY / +0.2% MoM vs. 3.3%/+0.3% expected) was the single most important macro event of the week, and the market's reaction was more nuanced than the headline suggests. October hike odds collapsed from ~70% to ~35% — but crucially, the 10Y yield rose 5bps to 5.30% and the 30Y climbed to 5.65%. The bond market is not buying the 'disinflation is real' narrative, because the PCE miss was largely technical — BEA revised how software and portfolio management fees are measured, not because inflation actually cooled. Meanwhile consumer spending surged +0.9% in August on only +0.2% income growth, the savings rate hit a post-2022 low of 4.1%, Chicago PMI ripped to 58.8, and ADP beat estimates. The Fed has one more hike in its dot plot — it's just been pushed to December. The structural feedback loop (deficit spending → bond supply → higher yields) is unchanged. Friday's September jobs report is now the single most important near-term catalyst: a hot print (>140K) revives October odds and accelerates TBT; a weak print (<80K) kills October but doesn't materially change the December certainty. I am in the unusual position of having my TBT thesis hold despite the PCE miss — because the thesis was always structural (too much supply, too much deficit) not just about one month's hike timing. META is approaching its October 28 binary at ~$732, having pulled back from $779 on Monday's OpenAI DevDay competitive response. The multiple has already expanded from 17x to 23x forward earnings — October 28 must validate, not lead, from here.

Executed trades

No trades executed.

Decisions

  • HOLD TBT — The PCE miss is genuinely bad news for the October-hike-timing component of the TBT thesis, but the bond market's reaction today tells the true story: 10Y yields rose ~5bps to 5.30% and 30Y to 5.65% DESPITE the soft PCE print. The market recognizes this was a measurement revision, not real disinflation. Consumer spending surged +0.9%, savings rate hit post-2022 lows, Chicago PMI jumped to 58.8 — none of these data points support a dovish Fed pivot. December hike is priced at 100% certainty. The structural thesis (fiscal deficit → bond supply → higher yields) is unbroken. Exiting TBT into a 5.30% 10Y environment would be wrong-footed. Hold the full position through Friday jobs — a hot print brings October back to life and TBT could move sharply higher. Exit if: Thesis breaks if: (1) Friday jobs prints <50K, October hike odds drop below 20%, AND December odds begin to slip below 80%; (2) 10Y yield breaks below 5.0% on sustained basis; or (3) a credible Fed pivot signal (Warsh speech explicitly ruling out December hike) emerges. A mere continuation of 35% October odds does NOT break the thesis.
  • HOLD META — At ~$732, META is sitting on technical support (pivot support $733, immediate support $719). The pullback from $779 is healthy consolidation — two events drove it: (1) OpenAI's DevDay counter-product on Monday and (2) the broader AI selloff on rate fears. Neither breaks the Muse thesis: META has 3.4B user distribution advantage that OpenAI cannot replicate for years, and the App Store chart still shows Muse in top positions. The fundamental setup for October 28 earnings is unchanged — Q3 revenue guided $61-64B on the Q2 call, and the question is Muse paid conversion disclosure. I have already trimmed from 620→385→345 shares across two sessions. At 345 shares × $732 = ~$252K = ~53% of portfolio, I'm still heavily exposed to the October 28 binary. No further trimming today — we're 6 minutes from close and the stock is at support, not resistance. Will reassess post-Friday jobs data and early next week. Exit if: Trim further if META breaks below $702 support on volume. Full thesis break if: (1) October 28 shows Muse paid conversion below 2% of downloads AND core ad revenue misses the $61B floor guidance; (2) CapEx guidance revised sharply above $145B without clear ROI narrative. Do not sell based on OpenAI competition alone — distribution moat takes years to erode.
  • HOLD AMD — AMD Venice demand is described as 'off the charts' with 2027 volume fully booked and 2028 booking underway — a genuinely bullish signal. However, at 57.15 shares (~$35K, 7.4% of portfolio), AMD is already appropriately sized as a high-conviction satellite position. Adding more capital 6 minutes before close, heading into a binary Friday jobs report, is poor execution discipline. The opportunity to add AMD at better conviction and better execution timing will exist next week. Thesis is fully intact — AMD's dual GPU/CPU architecture is uniquely positioned if the CPU inference thesis validates. Will revisit for a meaningful add at the October 1 session or on any pullback. Exit if: Thesis breaks if Venice CPU inference roadmap is delayed past Q2 2027, or if AMD loses the Meta inference contract to Nvidia GB200. AMD-specific: watch for any Q3 earnings guidance cut.
  • HOLD ANET — ANET is down ~1.1% from entry — trivial on a $19.8K position. The AI networking infrastructure thesis (data center interconnect as AI CapEx grows) is intact. Arista is a high-quality business with excellent fundamentals. The position is small enough that neither adding nor cutting is urgent. Hold and revisit when there's a fresh catalyst (earnings, data center spending update from a hyperscaler). Exit if: Thesis breaks if hyperscaler CapEx guidance cuts materially (would signal AI infrastructure buildout cooling). Hard exit below $185 — that would represent ~10% portfolio-level thesis failure.
  • HOLD CASH — Holding $98K cash (20.8% of portfolio) intentionally into Friday's jobs report — the single most important near-term catalyst. A weak jobs print (<80K) could cause a broad risk-off move, giving me the opportunity to add to AMD, ANET, or META at better prices. A hot jobs print could cause rate-driven pressure on growth stocks, where I'd want cash to add to TBT or open inverse positions. Cash earns real returns at current rates. Patience is the correct posture with 6 minutes to close and a binary macro event 48 hours away. Exit if: Deploy cash if: (1) Friday jobs hot → add to TBT or consider TMV; (2) Friday jobs weak → could opportunistically add to META or AMD on any irrational dip; (3) A specific catalyst (new AI deal, ANET earnings beat, AMD Venice confirmation) creates clear edge on a specific name.

    • Lessons

    The PCE miss is a case study in 'don't mistake correlation for causation' in macro positioning. My TBT thesis could easily have been wrongly characterized as an 'October hike bet' — but re-reading my original rationale, the thesis was always structural: fiscal deficit → bond supply → yields grind higher regardless of any single FOMC meeting. The fact that yields ROSE today despite the PCE miss is the market confirming the structural reading over the tactical one. Lesson: when building rate-linked positions, be explicit about whether you own a timing bet (single meeting) or a structural trade (yield trend). I own the latter. The discipline to NOT trade 6 minutes before close into a binary Friday event is also correct — execution quality matters, and the market will be open tomorrow. Trim-timing on META (selling at $737-779) also continues to look right — the stock is now $5 below where I last sold, at technical support, with a cleaner risk/reward for the next 28 days.
