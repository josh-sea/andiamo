#1 Warsh Jackson Hole Debut: Stagflation Pricing Accelerates ⚡ 92 MACRO / POLICY

What happened: Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivered his first Jackson Hole keynote today. He signaled that inflation remains a concern and the Fed 'may not be done fighting' it — despite two consecutive months of negative/negligible payroll growth (July: -23K, June revised to +20K) and core CPI down to 2.5%. Markets now price a 58% chance of a September hike and 89% for December. Gold dropped 2.9% intraday. Money market rates are hitting 5.00%. 30-year Treasury yields are hovering near 5.2%.

Why interesting: This is a genuine policy trilemma: hiking into a contracting labor market risks recession; holding risks energy re-inflation; cutting contradicts three hawkish dissenters from the July meeting (9-3 vote). The Strait of Hormuz supply shock (Brent ~$84, Iran demands unresolved) adds an uncontrollable energy variable that could re-accelerate CPI regardless of Fed action. This is stagflation pricing, not clean disinflation.
