This is not a normal rate-hike regime response — it suggests the market has re-classified AI capex as rate-insensitive, or alternatively, that duration sensitivity has been completely repriced out of growth names.

Key data points:
  • Fed funds rate: 3.75–4.00% as of Sept 16 hike
  • October hike probability: ~90% per Goldman Sachs
  • FOMC presser: Warsh described as terse, hawkish tone

  • Governor Waller struck a dovish counterpoint on Sept 21 — internal dissent signal

Why interesting: The Dow/Nasdaq inversion on a rate-hike day is historically anomalous and suggests deep sector-level repricing rather than macro-level fear.
