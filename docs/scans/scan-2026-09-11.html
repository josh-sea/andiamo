Summary: Beacon Pointe's 'Chart of the Month' flagged a structural threshold: US interest expense now consumes 19.7% of federal tax revenue — more than double its 2022 level, and now above the 17.9% spent on defense. The note is careful to say 'no imminent crisis' (dollar reserve status, deep capital markets), but the structural pressure on long-term rates is real.

Why interesting: This is a slow-burn signal that has historically preceded periods of fiscal-driven yield volatility. The 2022 UK gilt crisis (Truss moment) was a version of this mechanism accelerating rapidly. The US is not the UK, but the dynamic — fiscal dominance gradually constraining monetary policy — is worth tracking. If the Fed hikes again in September, it accelerates this flywheel. The OIG note flagged the same concern: USD weakness 'over the medium term as US fiscal dominance concerns build.' The spread between this concern and current 10Y yields is the trade.
