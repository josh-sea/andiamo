#1 Fed Credibility Crisis Under Warsh — The Long-End Bond Vigilante Test ⚡ 92 MACRO / RATES

What happened: Warsh held rates flat (7th consecutive hold, 3.50–3.75%) but made a strategic miscalculation: suggesting that rising market rates are doing the Fed's tightening for it. Markets heard that as evasion, not elegance. Result: aggressive bear-steepening. 30-yr Treasury hit 5.23% — highest since 2007. Critically, ~80% of the 10yr yield rise came from BREAKEVEN INFLATION, not real yields. Three FOMC dissents (Hammack, Logan, Kashkari) pressed for hikes. CNN is now running pieces saying the Fed's non-hikes were a mistake. If inflation re-accelerates into Q3, Warsh faces a binary: hike into slowing GDP (stagflation trap) or hold and lose the bond market.

Key question for deep dive: What does the breakeven inflation term structure look like? Is this a temporary spike or a regime shift in inflation expectations? Compare to May's yield spike.
